A Letter From Our Future 💌

To be opened whenever you wonder where we're going.

September 11, 2031 ❤️

Hey you ❤️


If you're reading this, I hope you're still the same person who can make me smile without even trying.


I don't know exactly what our life looks like five years from now. Maybe things turned out completely differently from what I imagined.


Maybe we've travelled to places we haven't even heard of yet. Maybe we're sitting somewhere together, arguing about something ridiculously unimportant.


But I hope one thing hasn't changed.


I still look at you sometimes and think, "How did I get this lucky?"


I hope we've collected hundreds of little memories by now — the silly ones, the difficult ones, the spontaneous ones, and the completely ordinary days that somehow became my favourites.


I don't want a perfect story with you.


I want our story.


The messy conversations. The stupid jokes. The random calls. The annoying habits. The hugs after an argument. The moments where neither of us says anything but somehow everything feels okay.


And if I could go back to September 11, 2026 and tell myself one thing, it would be:


Don't take her for granted. Appreciate the little things. And keep choosing her.


So wherever we are when you're reading this...


I hope I'm still annoying you.

I hope you're still stealing my attention.

And I hope, after all these years, you're still my favourite person.


Love,
Me — five years later ❤️
